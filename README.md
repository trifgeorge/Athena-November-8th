# Athena-November-8th
# Marathon Training Plan: November 8th Goal Race

**Weekly Structure:**
- **Monday:** Speed/Intervals (maintains your speed)
- **Wednesday:** Easy Run (building aerobic base)
- **Thursday:** Tempo/Threshold (builds stamina)
- **Saturday:** Long Run with Marathon Pace (the most important day)

---

## Pace Zones (Based on Your Current Fitness)

| Zone | Pace (per km) | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy (E)** | 5:35–6:00 | Recovery, building volume |
| **Marathon Pace (MP)** | 4:55–5:00 | Your goal race pace |
| **Tempo (T)** | 4:45–4:50 | Threshold, half-marathon pace |
| **Interval (I)** | 4:00–4:15 | 5K/10K speed, 800m–1km repeats |

---

## Phase Overview (15 Weeks)

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Building** | 1–4 | Rebuild endurance, establish routine |
| **Intensification** | 5–8 | Increase MP volume in long runs |
| **Peak** | 9–12 | Maximum volume, race rehearsal |
| **Taper** | 13–15 | Reduce volume, maintain sharpness |

## Pace + Heart Rate Zones (Quick Reference)

| Zone | Pace (per km) | BPM Range | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy (E)** | 5:35–6:00 | 140–148 | Recovery, building volume |
| **Marathon Pace (MP)** | 4:55–5:00 | 159–165 | Your goal race pace |
| **Tempo (T)** | 4:45–4:50 | 165–172 | Threshold, half-marathon pace |
| **Interval (I)** | 4:00–4:15 | 174–183 | 5K/10K speed |

## Final Advice

1. **Don't skip the long runs.** They are the most important workout in this plan.

2. **Listen to your body.** If you feel overly fatigued, swap an interval day for an easy run.

3. **Nutrition matters.** Practice fueling on every long run over 25km.

4. **Trust the taper.** You will feel restless in Weeks 14–15. That's normal. Your fitness is already there.
